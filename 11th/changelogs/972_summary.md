Points and weapon updates to Space Marines and 6 other factions

**Adeptus Astartes**
- Assault Intercessor Squad: decreased points (175→170, 90→85)
- Bladeguard Veteran Squad: increased points (170→180, 85→90)
- Desolation Squad: increased points (135→140)
- Gladiator Lancer: decreased points (165→160)
- Heavy Intercessor Squad: increased points (220→230, 110→115)
- Inceptor Squad: increased points (250→260, 125→130)
- Intercessor Squad: decreased points (175→170, 95→85)
- Redemptor Dreadnought: decreased points (180→170)
- Repulsor Executioner: decreased points (275→260)
- Terminator Squad: decreased points (390→380, 195→190)
- Vanguard Veteran Squad with Jump Packs: decreased points (240→220, 120→110)
- and 31 other minor changes

**Blood Angels**
- Death Company Captain: "Power Fist" increased Attacks (3→6) and improved BS/WS (3+→2+)

**Space Wolves**
- Logan Grimnar: "Storm Bolter" moved from melee to ranged weapons
- Thunderwolf Cavalry: Storm Shield wargear option increased in cost (0→5)
- Wolf Guard Headtakers: decreased points (170→115, 115→85); gained "Infantry", "Tacticus" and "Beast" keywords
- Wolf Guard Terminators: Storm Shield wargear option increased in cost (0→5)

**Grey Knights**
- Strike Squad: "Storm Bolter" decreased Attacks (3→2)
- Brotherhood Terminator Squad: "Storm Bolter" decreased Attacks (3→2)
- and 9 other datasheets with "Storm Bolter" decreased Attacks (3→2)

**Adeptus Custodes**
- Anathema Psykana Rhino: "Storm Bolter" decreased Attacks (3→2)

**Agents of the Imperium**
- Grey Knights Terminator Squad: "Storm Bolter" decreased Attacks (3→2)
- Imperial Rhino: "Storm Bolter" decreased Attacks (3→2)

**Emperor's Children**
- Tormentors: increased Toughness (4→5)
